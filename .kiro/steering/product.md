# Product: Crypt Keeper

Crypt Keeper is a haunted deadline tracker for students. Every assignment is a ghost. As a due date approaches, the ghost drifts toward the Keeper's lantern and its face turns from calm to angry. Past the deadline it becomes a wraith and drains the Keeper's sanity. Finishing the work banishes the ghost.

## Who it is for
University students juggling several courses who ignore plain to-do lists. The spooky metaphor makes time pressure visible at a glance.

## Principles
- The spooky design must carry real information. Distance from the lantern means time left. Ghost size means effort. Ghost colour means course. Sanity is a live summary of how overdue you are.
- Never trade clarity for atmosphere. Every ghost has a readable label, and the Ledger view gives a plain sorted list of the same data.
- No accounts, no backend. Data lives in the browser and can be exported as JSON or .ics.
- Respect the user: sound is optional, motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`, and notifications need explicit consent.

## Voice
Short, dry, a little theatrical. Say "Banish" instead of "Complete", "Summon" instead of "Add", "Tombstones" instead of "History". Never use jokes that make the user feel bad about missed deadlines.

## Out of scope
Accounts, cloud sync, collaboration, and AI features. Do not add them without an updated spec.
